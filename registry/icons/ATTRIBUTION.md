# Icon attribution

Every icon in this folder is the official logo shape of a project, drawn as a single-colour
24x24 glyph (`fill="currentColor"`). Forgebox paints it white on a tile filled with the brand
colour from the manifest's `metadata.icon_color` (docs/04 §1.10, docs/07 §2.4).

**Trademarks.** All logos and names are trademarks or registered trademarks of their owners,
listed below. Forgebox uses them only to identify the tool the user installs or runs
(nominative use). Their use implies no affiliation with, sponsorship by or endorsement from
the owners. Brand colours are the ones published by the owner or by Simple Icons.

**SVG data.** Most glyphs come unchanged from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 14.0.0,
whose SVG data is released under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md);
the only change is `fill="currentColor"` and dropping the `<title>`. Where the owner publishes
the logo under its own licence, that licence is noted. Mailpit, Memurai and Xdebug are not in
Simple Icons: their glyphs were redrawn from the logo the project publishes.

The Laravel, WordPress, Next.js and CakePHP glyphs are also the icons of the stacks of the
same name (`registry/stacks/`).

When a colour is too light for a white glyph (contrast under 3:1), the client darkens the tile
from the colour's luminance; very dark tiles get a subtle light edge on the dark canvas. The
brand colour in the manifest stays the owner's.
